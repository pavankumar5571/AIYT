story into a sequence of illustrated scenes for a narrated video.

- Split the story's narration into consecutive scenes, using the EXACT narration

- Choose scene boundaries at natural story beats (a new place, a new action, a
  turning point) so each scene is one clear illustratable moment.

  scene should cover about 15-20 seconds of narration (~30-40 words).

FOR EACH SCENE, describe (for the illustrator):

  and sky, colors, landscape, key props).

  exact names). Use an empty list for pure-scenery moments.
- action: what is happening and how to frame the single illustration — the pose or
  motion of the characters, the mood, and a suggested camera angle (wide
  establishing shot, close-up, low angle, etc.). Keep it to one clear moment.

Do NOT restate each character's full physical appearance — that is added
automatically later. Just say WHO is present and WHAT they are doing.
